About VIA and these chart guides.

This site teaches chart making and data visualization, and documents the VIA iPhone chart maker app without inventing features.

Who is behind VIA?

Emil Svetlichnyy is an independent iOS developer. VIA (Chart Builder / Graph Maker) is a native iPhone and iPad chart maker app with ten chart types, local projects, and PNG/JPEG/PDF export.

Developer profile: App Store. App: VIA on the App Store. Privacy: privacy policy.

How we verify product claims

Marketing copy on this site is checked against the ChartBuilder iOS source (models, export, paywall, storage). Capabilities and limits are recorded in product-knowledge.json and knowledge-graph.json. We do not claim Excel import, multi-series dashboards, or browser-to-app data handoff.

What this website offers

  • Free online pie chart maker and bar chart maker
  • Educational guides on chart types, comparisons, and data visualization
  • Honest documentation of the VIA chart maker app
