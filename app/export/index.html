Export from VIA

VIA exports through the iOS share sheet and can save images to Photos. Formats and Pro gates below match the app implementation.

Download on theApp Store App hub

Formats

PNG, JPEG (quality 0.9), and PDF. Background options: White, Transparent, Dark. Size presets from 640×480 to 3840×2160.

Pro export gate

Pro required to export: Stacked, Scatter, Radar, Gantt.
Free-tier export works for Pie, Donut, Line, Area, Bar, and H. Bar.
Free users can still select and edit Pro chart types; the gate is export.

Other free limits

One saved project on the free tier.
Up to three saved custom colors on the free tier.

Questions

Can I export PDF?

Yes, from VIA.

Which types need Pro to export?

Stacked, Scatter, Radar, and Gantt.

Can I save to Photos?

Yes, after granting Photos permission.
