Horizontal bar chart — H. Bar in VIA.

A horizontal bar chart compares categories with bars drawn left to right. In VIA the type is labeled H. Bar. Use it when labels are long. Browser tools on this site cover vertical bars only.

What is a horizontal bar chart?

A horizontal bar chart encodes values as bar length along a horizontal axis — the same comparison job as a vertical bar chart, with room for longer category names.

When should you use it?

Category names are long (survey answers, product names).
You are ranking items by size.
For parts of one whole, prefer pie or donut.
