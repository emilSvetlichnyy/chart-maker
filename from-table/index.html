Make a chart from a table

You need two columns: a label and a value. Copy them into the online pie or bar maker, then download a PNG — or finish in VIA on iPhone.

Pie maker Bar maker Download on the App Store

Steps

  1. Keep two columns: names and numbers.
  2. Choose pie for shares or bar to compare.
  3. Copy the values into the maker (or type them on your phone).
  4. Download a PNG, or open VIA if you need saved charts and line graphs.

Common blockers

On phones, selecting non-adjacent Excel columns is often hard — copy the two you need.
Remove currency symbols if the maker expects plain numbers.
One chart should answer one question. Don’t dump a whole workbook into one visual.

Questions

Can I chart Google Sheets data on my phone?

Yes. Copy the label and value columns into the pie or bar maker, or enter them in VIA.

What if my columns aren’t next to each other?

Copy each column into a clean two-column block first. That’s usually faster than fighting mobile Excel selection.

Do I need an account?

Not for the website makers. VIA on the App Store can be started without a long setup.
