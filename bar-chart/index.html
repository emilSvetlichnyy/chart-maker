When to use a bar chart

Use bars when the question is “which is bigger?”
Keep units the same on one chart (don’t mix % and dollars).
For parts of one whole, a pie chart is usually clearer.

Questions

Bar chart or pie chart?

Bar chart to compare amounts. Pie chart for shares of one total.
