Fast path for school charts

Most homework charts are simple: 3–6 categories or a short time series. You do not need a full spreadsheet app for that.

Pick pie for shares (budget, survey), bar for compare (scores, cities), line for change over time, radar for multi-skill scores.

Practical tips for clear homework charts

Label every slice or bar clearly — avoid unlabeled colors.
Keep one question per chart.
Export PNG at a readable size for Google Docs or slides.
