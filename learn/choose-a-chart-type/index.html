Decision guide

Choose a chart type.

Pick the visual from the question you need answered — not from which icon looks familiar.

Quick answer

Part-to-whole → pie/donut. Ranking categories → bar or H. Bar. Trend over time → line/area. Two numeric variables → scatter. Multi-attribute profile → radar. Task schedule → gantt.

Decision rules

Parts of one total — Pie or donut. Keep 3–6 slices. Pie vs bar
Compare / rank categories — Bar; use H. Bar for long labels.
Change over a sequence — Line; use area when filled volume helps. Line vs area
Relationship between two numbers — Scatter (X and Y).
Profile across attributes — Radar (also called spider). Prefer bars if exact ranking matters. Radar vs bar
Task dates — Gantt for start/end timelines.

Types at a glance

Pie

Parts of one whole; 3–6 categories

Donut

Same jobs as pie; When a center total helps

Line

Trends over time; Continuous sequences

Area

Volume over time; Single series emphasis

Bar

Compare categories; Rank values

Horizontal bar

Long category names; Ranked lists

Stacked bar

Composition inside a total; Part-to-whole with named segments

Scatter

Relationship between two numeric variables; Outlier detection

Radar

Multi-attribute profiles; Skill or score comparisons

Gantt

Project timelines; Task duration and overlap

How do I choose a chart type in a chart maker?

Before you open a free chart maker or graph maker, match the question to a chart type: pie chart for shares, bar chart for ranking, line chart for trends, scatter for relationships, radar for profiles, gantt for timelines.

This decision guide works with any data visualization tool. On this site, use the online pie chart maker or bar chart maker; for other chart types, use the VIA chart maker app on iPhone.

FAQ

How do I choose a chart type?

Start from the question: part-to-whole, ranking, trend, relationship, profile, or timeline — then pick the matching type.

Pie or bar?

Pie for shares of one total with few categories; bar for comparing magnitudes. See the pie vs bar guide.

What does VIA support?

VIA includes pie, donut, line, area, bar, H. Bar, stacked, scatter, radar, and gantt. Browser tools on this site cover pie and bar only.
