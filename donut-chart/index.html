What is a donut chart?

A donut chart shows parts of a whole, like a pie chart, but leaves the center empty. That hole is a good place for a total, a percentage, or a category name.

Rules are the same as pie: keep 3–6 slices, group the rest as Other, and switch to a bar chart if you are comparing sizes across groups.

Pie vs donut

Same data model: labels + values that add to one total.

Questions

Is a donut chart the same as a pie chart?

Same idea — parts of a whole. A donut leaves the center open for a total or label.

How many slices should a donut chart have?

Usually 3–6. More slices get hard to read on a phone.
