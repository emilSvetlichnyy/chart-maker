What is a area chart?

An area chart plots values in sequence and fills the region under the line so magnitude over time is easier to see than with a plain line alone.

When should you use it?

You care about change over time and overall volume under the curve.
You have an ordered series (days, weeks, months).
For category comparison at one moment, use a bar chart instead.

What is an area chart?

An area chart is a line chart with the region under the line filled. Use it when magnitude/volume matters as much as direction in your data visualization.

Questions

Is there a free online area chart maker here?

No. This site’s live tools are pie and bar only. Area charts are in the VIA iOS app.

Does VIA support area charts?

Yes. Area is one of the ten chart types in VIA.

Area chart or line chart?

Both show trends. Area fills under the line; Line shows the path only.

Can I export an area chart?

Yes — PNG, JPEG, or PDF from VIA. Area export is available without the Pro export gate.

Do I need Excel?

No. Enter values directly in VIA.
