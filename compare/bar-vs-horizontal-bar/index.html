Bar Chart vs Horizontal Bar Chart

Vertical bars are familiar for short labels. Horizontal bars (H. Bar in VIA) win when category names are long or you want an easy ranked list.

Quick answer

Vertical bars are familiar for short labels. Horizontal bars (H. Bar in VIA) win when category names are long or you want an easy ranked list.

Decision rule: If labels wrap or truncate, switch to horizontal bars.

Definitions

Bar: A chart that encodes values as vertical bar lengths so categories can be compared by eye along a shared baseline.

Horizontal bar: A bar chart with bars drawn horizontally, preferred when category labels are long or ranking needs easy top-to-bottom reading.

Common mistakes

Truncated y-axis exaggeration
Sorting randomly when rank matters
Using vertical bars with truncated labels
