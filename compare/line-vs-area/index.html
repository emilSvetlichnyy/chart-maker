Line Chart vs Area Chart

Both show trends. A line emphasizes path and slope; an area chart fills under the line to emphasize magnitude and volume of change.

Which chart type fits your data visualization?

Choosing between a line chart and a area chart is a common chart maker decision. Use the quick answer and table below, then open the matching free online tool or the VIA graph maker on iPhone.

Quick answer

Both show trends. A line emphasizes path and slope; an area chart fills under the line to emphasize magnitude and volume of change.

Decision rule: Prefer line for clarity with multiple series; prefer area for a single series where filled volume aids storytelling.

Definitions

Line: A chart that connects ordered values with a line to emphasize change, direction, and rate over a sequence such as time.

Area: A line chart with the region under the line filled to emphasize magnitude of change as well as direction.

Common mistakes

Connecting unordered categories
Too many overlapping series
Using area for categorical ranking
Implying cumulative totals incorrectly
