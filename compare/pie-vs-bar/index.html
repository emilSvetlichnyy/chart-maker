Pie Chart vs Bar Chart

Use a pie chart for parts of one whole with few categories. Use a bar chart to compare magnitudes across categories, especially when labels are many or values are close.

Quick answer

Use a pie chart for parts of one whole with few categories. Use a bar chart to compare magnitudes across categories, especially when labels are many or values are close.

Decision rule: If categories must sum to 100% and you have 3–6 slices, prefer pie (or donut). If you need ranking or precise comparison, prefer bar.

Side-by-side

JobPieBar

Definitions

Pie: A circular chart that divides a whole into slices whose angles are proportional to each category’s share of a single total.

Bar: A chart that encodes values as vertical bar lengths so categories can be compared by eye along a shared baseline.

Common mistakes

Too many slices
3D pie distortion
Using pie when totals differ across groups
Truncated y-axis exaggeration
