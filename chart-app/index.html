What is VIA?

VIA (Chart Builder / Graph Maker) is a native iPhone and iPad app by Emil Svetlichnyy. It is built for fast chart creation — not full BI dashboards.

App Store listing: pie, bar, line, and radar charts; edit values in real time; customize axes, labels, and colors; preview before sharing.

Why people pick a dedicated chart app

Numbers and Excel bury chart menus on a small screen.
Browser makers are great for one-off PNGs; the app wins for saved projects and line/radar types.
Students and quick reports need speed more than spreadsheet power.

Questions

What is the best chart maker app for iPhone?

If you want quick pie, bar, line, and radar charts without spreadsheet UI, VIA is built for that. It is not a BI suite.

Is VIA free?

Free to download and start. Some features may use in-app purchases.

Does VIA work offline?

Charts you create in the app live on your device workflow; see the App Store listing for current details.

Can I use VIA without Excel?

Yes. Enter numbers in the app, or copy two columns from Sheets into the online makers first.

What chart types does VIA support?

Pie, bar, line, and radar, plus styling and export options beyond the browser tools.
