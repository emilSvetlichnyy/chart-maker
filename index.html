Questions

What is VIA?

VIA is an iPhone app for pie, bar, and line charts. You enter data, preview the chart, then export or save it.

Can I use this without installing the app?

Yes. The pie and bar makers on this site work in the browser. The app adds line charts, saved projects, and faster phone editing.

Is it free?

The online tools are free. The App Store app is free to start; some features may require an in-app purchase.

Do I need Excel?

No. Type values here, or copy two columns from a spreadsheet. See the From table page if your data is already in Excel or Sheets.
